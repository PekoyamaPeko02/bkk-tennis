## Brand & Style
The design system embodies athletic energy balanced with high-end club prestige. It delivers an immediate sense of performance, tropical court atmosphere, and effortless spatial discovery for Bangkok’s tennis community—spanning local enthusiasts, expat players, and competitive club members.

The visual tone sits at the intersection of **High-Contrast Sporty** and **Modern Functionalism**. High-luminance chartreuse optics provide kinetic punch and immediate focal pull, grounded by deep forest green and midnight slate that suggest premier country clubs and evening floodlit matches. Soft clay terracotta injects warm tactical distinction for status indicators and high-impact calls to action. The aesthetic is clean, fast, and structured, prioritizing rapid geographic scanning, instantaneous court availability checks, and streamlined slot reservations under intense urban heat.

## Colors
The palette balances athletic vibrancy with editorial structure:

- **Primary (`#064E3B`)**: Deep Forest Green serves as the primary brand foundation—used for key app navigation bars, primary headings, dominant brand surfaces, and high-emphasis structural cards.
- **Secondary (`#84CC16` with `#A3E635` highlights)**: Tennis Ball Volt/Electric Chartreuse acts as the primary functional accent. It is reserved for high-visibility highlights, active GPS radius rings, confirmed slot selections, availability badges, and interactive map pins.
- **Tertiary (`#EA580C`)**: Clay Terracotta references iconic court surfaces and is deployed for urgent status indicators (e.g., "Only 1 Slot Left", live alerts, weather status, and special club promotions).
- **Neutral (`#0F172A`)**: Midnight Slate provides razor-sharp text contrast, borders, and footer elements, preventing pure `#000000` harshness.
- **Canvas & Surface System**: Backing canvases use crisp neutral white (`#FFFFFF`) with cool neutral tinting (`#F8FAFC` to `#F1F5F9`) for secondary cards, distance clusters, and filter panels.

## Typography
The system uses a geometric dual-type architecture:

- **Headlines & Metric Data (`Space Grotesk`)**: Provides an athletic, technical edge reminiscent of tournament scoreboards, court dimensions, and split-second precision.
- **Body & Controls (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Delivers geometric clarity and natural reading comfort across both English and Thai scripts. Plus Jakarta Sans handles Thai character accents and vowel marks smoothly alongside Latin characters, ensuring baseline stability across bilingual venue titles, district names (e.g., Sukhumvit, Thong Lo, Bang Na), and operational details.
- Numbers such as distances (km), hourly rates (฿), and court availability times always utilize `Space Grotesk` with tabular figures where applicable to prevent layout shifting during real-time GPS updates.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout relies on a dynamic split-pane and fluid grid structure designed for dual-context exploration: interactive map discovery alongside list/card booking flows.

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: A 12-column hybrid layout. In discovery mode, a 5-column sticky list coordinates with a 7-column persistent interactive vector map. In booking schedule view, a 12-column full canvas with a central 1200px max-width container is used.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: An 8-column layout. The map toggles via a floating bottom pill ("View Map" vs. "View List") or operates in a top 40vh map / bottom 60vh scrollable sheet configuration.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: A 4-column layout utilizing bottom-sheet drawers for venue filters, court specs, and checkout sheets. Gutters drop to 12px (`0.75rem`), with 16px (`1rem`) edge margins to maximize interactive target sizes on touch screens.
- **Rhythm**: Component spacing follows an 8px base grid. Tight spatial relations inside badges and micro-pills leverage 4px (`space-xs`).

## Elevation & Depth
Depth hierarchy avoids heavy synthetic drop shadows, leaning instead into crisp court boundary aesthetics, tactical surface layering, and luminous ambient glows:

- **Level 0 (Court Ground)**: Flat base surfaces (`#FFFFFF` and `#F8FAFC`) with razor-fine hairline borders (`1px solid #E2E8F0`).
- **Level 1 (Card & Slot Tiles)**: Elevated by `0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 1px 2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)` combined with a subtle neutral rim (`1px solid #E2E8F0`). Hover states shift the border color to `#064E3B` and increase elevation slightly.
- **Level 2 (Popovers, Sticky Bar, Floating Filters)**: Lifted using `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(6, 78, 59, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`.
- **Active & Kinetic Elements**: Map pin highlights and active reservation slots emit a 4px soft energy glow using secondary chartreuse: `0 0 16px rgba(132, 204, 22, 0.45)`.
- **Overlays & Drawers**: Bangkok heat-map layers and time-slot drawers use subtle backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) with `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.6)`.

## Shapes
The shape philosophy incorporates precision athletic curves:

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem` / `8px`)**: Form inputs, court thumbnail imagery, filter tags, and operational buttons.
- **Large Radius (`1rem` / `16px`)**: Venue overview cards, booking modal sheets, and court surface selection panels.
- **Pill Geometry (`9999px`)**: Reserved exclusively for dynamic status markers: distance badges (e.g., "1.4 km away"), availability pills ("3 Courts Free"), surface tags ("Clay", "Hard Court", "Plexicushion"), and floating action buttons.
- **Map Pins**: Teardrop geometries morphing into rounded rectangular badges upon selection to display venue pricing and live walking/driving distance.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Athletic Button**: Solid Deep Forest Green (`#064E3B`) with white text and bold weight (`Plus Jakarta Sans 600`). On hover, background deepens to `#04392B` with a subtle translate-y shift. Focus states display a 2px offset ring in `#84CC16`.
- **Volt Action Button**: High-impact CTA for "Book Now" and instant confirmation. Solid Tennis Chartreuse (`#84CC16`) with Midnight Slate (`#0F172A`) typography for optimal legibility.
- **Secondary / Ghost**: White surface with a 1.5px border in `#CBD5E1`, transitioning to `#064E3B` border and text on interaction.

### Chips & Availability Pills
- **Real-Time Availability**: Pill-shaped with a pulse indicator. Green state uses a soft mint background (`#ECFDF5`), deep green text (`#064E3B`), and an animated chartreuse dot.
- **Low Availability / Clay Alert**: Warm terracotta tint (`#FFEDD5`) with deep orange text (`#C2410C`) for limited slot warnings.
- **Surface Filter Chips**: Interactive toggle pills displaying surface type and indoor/covered status icons. Inactive chips use neutral slate lines; active chips shift to `#064E3B` fill with crisp white text.

### GPS & Distance Badges
- Compact pills anchored to card imagery and map nodes. Dark translucent backing (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.8)`) with white text, featuring an electric chartreuse GPS location icon and numeric distance formatted to 1 decimal place.

### Time Slot Matrix (Booking Engine)
- Horizontal grid tiles representing 30 and 60-minute increments.
- *Available*: Crisp white container, `#E2E8F0` border, `#0F172A` time label.
- *Selected*: Background `#064E3B`, text `#FFFFFF`, sub-label in `#A3E635`.
- *Unavailable / Past*: Light gray fill (`#F1F5F9`), strike-through typography, non-interactive cursor.

### Cards (Court & Club Discovery)
- Divided into image container (16:10 aspect ratio) with overlay distance and surface tags, followed by a structured content section.
- Content rows display club name (Space Grotesk), Thai neighborhood subtitle (e.g., "วัฒนา / Watthana"), lighting capability icon (Floodlit), and pricing per hour prominently right-aligned.

### Form Inputs & Date Pickers
- High-contrast 44px minimum touch targets with internal icons (search, calendar, player count). Inactive borders sit at `#CBD5E1`, focusing to a distinct 2px `#064E3B` ring. Bilingual placeholder labels toggle or stack cleanly (e.g., "Select Date / เลือกวันที่").
